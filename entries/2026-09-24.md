**FEATURE — Switching effort mid-conversation on Opus 5.5 no longer wipes your prompt cache.** Source: robonuggets@mail.beehiiv.com, "12 Hacks to Prompt Opus 5.5 the RIGHT Way," 2026-09-24, citing Anthropic's own prompting guide (YouTube: "Claude has Evolved — 12 NEW Rules for Opus 5.5 by Anthropic Engineers"). On prior models, changing effort mid-chat reset the cache and reprocessed the whole context at full cost; on Opus 5.5 the cache persists across an effort change. Other guide tips: give Opus 5.5 an explicit time budget ("finish this in 3 minutes") to make it pace itself, and give it a concrete design system or a list of styles to avoid — asking for "less generic" alone just swaps one generic default for another.

**WORKFLOW — CoreWeave's product/engineering EVP Chen Goldberg: long-running agents break the assumptions cloud infrastructure was built on.** Source: theneuron@newsletter.theneurondaily.com, "CoreWeave: AI infrastructure is one giant computer," 2026-09-23, youtu.be/QGYS7aiCQHU (podcast, 16:57 mark).
