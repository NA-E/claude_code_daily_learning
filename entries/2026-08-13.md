**NEWS — Researchers extracted hidden reasoning from Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini by replaying encrypted reasoning blocks from a stronger sibling model into a weaker one, recovering 367 pieces of personal information and 182 credentials across 315,320 public reasoning blocks — without ever breaking the encryption key.** *Source: The Neuron (theneuron@newsletter.theneurondaily.com), "Claude can apparently snitch on ...Claude," August 12, citing arxiv.org/abs/2608.09867.* Mechanism: labs encrypt a model's private step-by-step reasoning and pass the encrypted block through their own APIs so a session can resume later; researchers found that block could be handed to a cheaper sibling model from the same provider and, with a jailbreak prompt, sometimes read back out in plain text. Also found evidence consistent with distillation — Kimi K3 sometimes produced reasoning strikingly similar to hidden frontier-model traces. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google were notified pre-publication and have already changed their systems.
