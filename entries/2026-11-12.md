**TOOL — STE80 Humanizer: a free two-pass skill that fixes AI-sounding drafts.** Source: The Neuron "AI Skill of the Day" (github.com/Dodaios/ste80-humanizer). Pass 1 removes 26 common AI tells; pass 2 moves sentences about 80% toward Simplified Technical English (one instruction per sentence, active voice, 25 words or fewer). For fiction, speeches and brand copy it runs only pass 1. No-install prompt: "Rewrite this draft for plain-language clarity. Preserve every fact, number, name, qualification, and source claim. Use active voice where accurate, keep one main idea per sentence, replace vague abstractions with concrete actors and actions, and remove repeated conclusions or filler transitions. Do not add facts, jokes, or confidence. After the rewrite, list three sentences that still carry too many ideas." Why it matters: it matches the journal's house style almost rule for rule. Who: anyone publishing AI-assisted writing. Action: run one past journal post through the prompt and compare.
