**WORKFLOW — Nate Parrott's five ways to get Claude designs without AI slop.** Source: Peter Yang (podcast, 42 min, "How to Get Claude to Design Without AI Slop in 40 Minutes"). (1) Give context first: screenshots, pages you like, your past work; for an existing product, have Claude rebuild the real screens from code. (2) For hard layouts, have Claude research familiar products that solve a similar problem. (3) Tell Claude its own biases ("The model has no idea what its own biases are"): in `design.md`, discourage serif-only text, off-white backgrounds and card-edge accent bars. (4) Start with wireframes and fix the core flows before fonts and colors. (5) Ask for 5 options per key screen, then remix the parts you like into 5 more. The episode also covers the "4 tells" of an AI-made design (18:55), turning a spreadsheet into a live dashboard (22:36), then into a Claude Motion video (26:17), and how to test a design skill when AI can't judge taste (31:11). Why it matters: it comes from the person who designs Claude's own design tools. Who: anyone shipping Claude-built pages, including nourin.dev. Action: see Top Thing.

## Top Thing to Implement Right Now

**ONE ACTION → add a "Claude design biases" block to your design file → DO TODAY.**

**HIGH LEVERAGE:** Parrott's point is that Claude does not know its own design habits, so it repeats them on every page. Writing them down once fixes every future page, nourin.dev and the journal included. It is the design version of Every's finding (11-14) that real examples beat a style rule written in one pass.

Create or open `design.md` (or the design section of `CLAUDE.md`) in your site repo and paste:

```markdown
## Avoid Claude's default design tells
- No serif-only typography. Pair one sans for UI with at most one display face.
- No off-white / cream page backgrounds unless the brand calls for it.
- No colored accent bars on card edges.
- Before styling, rebuild the existing screens from code and match them.
- Wireframe first. Do not pick fonts or colors until the core flow is approved.
- For any key screen, give 5 distinct options (font, color, layout), then remix my picks into 5 more.
- For hard layouts, name 2–3 familiar products that solve the same problem and follow their pattern.
```

Then test it: ask Claude Code to redesign one existing page with and without the block, and keep the version you would publish.
