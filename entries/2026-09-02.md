**MODEL — Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1, its new top model for coding, research, and long-running agents.** Source: no-reply@email.claude.com (official Anthropic announcement), 2026-09-01, anthropic.com/introducing-claude-fable-mythos-5-1. **FEATURE — Cache-read pricing for Fable 5.1 dropped 75%, from $1 to $0.25 per million tokens, while input/output pricing stays at $10/$50 per million.** Source: alexmcfarland.substack.com, 2026-09-01, "Claude Fable 5.1 is here—and so is watermarked AI text." Anthropic estimates this makes a typical workload roughly 25% cheaper and a highly agentic one (mostly cache hits) up to 45% cheaper. Caveat from the same source: Max-plan weekly limits still treat Fable models the same as before — Fable 5.1 can burn up to half your weekly allowance before you're switched off it, so the API savings and your subscription limit are two separate things.

**FEATURE — Fable 5.1 adds per-message effort control (beta), letting an agent change reasoning effort mid-conversation without invalidating the prompt cache.** Source: no-reply@email.claude.com, platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/effort. Anthropic recommends running routine steps at Low or Medium effort and raising to High only for ambiguous decisions or final verification — a Sonnet 5 subagent, for example, could plan at High, execute at Medium, and verify at High, all in one cached conversation.

**CONCEPT — Every Claude model released after August 2, 2026, including Fable 5.1, now embeds an imperceptible statistical watermark in its text output, and Anthropic requires new organizations to preserve thinking blocks unmodified for anti-distillation reasons.** Source: no-reply@email.claude.com and alexmcfarland.substack.com, both 2026-09-01. The watermark changes the model's token-sampling randomness rather than adding visible markup; it carries no identifying data about you or your org. A detection API is in private preview for EU-eligible regulators, media, and compliance teams — docs at platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/preserved-thinking.

**WORKFLOW — theneuralmaze.substack.com closed its six-week OCR course by wiring the cluster into Claude Code over MCP, and flagged that OCR output is untrusted text feeding straight into a code-writing agent.** Source: theneuralmaze.substack.com, 2026-09-02, github.com/neural-maze/production-ocr-course.
