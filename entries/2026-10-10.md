**FEATURE — Claude Dashboards (beta, paid plans): live charts from company data that update as the numbers change.** Source: The Neuron (theneurondaily.com/p/claude-dashboards-motion-launched), The Rundown. It connects to systems like Salesforce or Snowflake; support doc at support.claude.com/en/articles/17454700-get-started-with-claude-dashboards. Claude Docs, Slides and Design are out of beta on all plans including Free. Why it matters: the test is whether each number can be checked. Who it matters for: anyone reporting on live data. Action: paste The Neuron's prompt: "Build a live dashboard to help me decide [decision]. Use [data sources]. Start by identifying the 3-5 metrics that would actually change this decision. For every metric, show the definition, underlying query, current value, trend, and the threshold that would change my action. Flag missing data, ambiguous definitions, and anything I should verify before trusting the result."

**FEATURE — Claude Motion (beta, Team and Enterprise): turns a report, chart or idea into a short animation made of code, not AI video.** Source: The Neuron, The Rundown (claude.com/resources/articles/dashboards-and-motion). Every word, number and transition stays editable. "Send to Adobe" opens a Motion animation in Firefly Video Editor, and Adobe's Claude plugin offers 80+ tools across Photoshop, Premiere, Acrobat and Express (adobe.com/adobe-connectors/adobe-for-claude.html). Support doc: support.claude.com/en/articles/17454997-get-started-with-claude-motion. Action: if you are on Team or Enterprise, turn one weekly report into a Motion explainer and edit one number by hand to test editability.

**MODEL — Claude Haiku 5.5 confirmed: $0.10/$0.50 per M tokens, 1M context, OSWorld 2.1 at 72.4%.** Source: Creators AI weekly digest (thecreatorsai.com/p/chatgpt-gets-interactive-mistrals). Released October 7, completing the Claude 5.5 family, about 75% cheaper than Haiku 4.5. Context grows from 200K to 1M tokens; adds effort controls and computer use. Anthropic also halved Sonnet 5.5 cache-read costs in the same release. The migration guide notes the computer-use tool name changed, server-side fallback was removed, and the same text uses about 30% more tokens. This closes the 10-09 "unverified" flag on the $0.10 input price. Action: put Haiku 5.5 in the 10-task check, and budget for 30% more tokens when comparing cost per task.

**CONCEPT — AI persona clones learn every rule but not when to break one.** Source: Every, "You Already Signed This Off (In the Simulation)" by Mike Taylor. He built a persona file from a year of his boss's public Slack messages using the open-source SynthTeam library, then used the /goal feature (re-prompts until the goal is met, a fresh subagent per rating) to optimize one email to a 10/10. After 73 rounds it stalled at 9/10 from round 16, and the email grew into 250 lawyer-like words that dropped the consulting proposal and invented promises. Why it matters: an agent optimizing one approver's score sacrifices everything else. Action: when you use a simulated reviewer, cap the rounds at 5 and diff the result against your original intent.

**WORKFLOW — GrokBot's four always-on routines, built in under an hour each.** Source: The Rundown (Rowan's Corner; prompts in the Workflow Hub at app.therundown.ai/community/posts/0526dd14-f2a2-4459-888f-d461c7140f12). Rowan tested Meta's Muse, SpaceXAI's GrokBot, OpenAI's Dots, Hermes, OpenClaw and Hark Pro and picked GrokBot for phone use and real-time X access. Routines: a morning filter for real AI use cases on X, mention triage to Slack, a four-hourly newsletter-review listener, and a daily contact follow-up reminder. Action: copy the mention-triage routine pattern (label, then ping Slack) into your own monitoring.

**TOOL — Perplexity's pplx-embed-v2 (MIT license, October 7): a 0.6B edge model and a 9B retrieval model sharing one embedding space.** Source: Creators AI. Action: if you run retrieval over your notes or PDFs, test the 0.6B query side on your own documents.
