**CONCEPT — Playbooks make cheaper models work: a written procedure raised success from about 1-in-3 to about 1-in-2 across 87 tasks, and AI-written playbooks did worse than none.** Source: theaiexchange@mail.beehiiv.com, "We moved our AI to a cheaper model. Three steps: run a written playbook on a cheaper model five times, compare, switch if it holds. Also notes Microsoft's new Copilot moved to pay-per-use pricing (fortune.com/2026/09/25).
