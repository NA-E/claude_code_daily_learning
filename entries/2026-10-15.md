**CONCEPT — Runway Solaris, an "interface world model."** Source: The Neuron (runway.com/news/research/introducing-solaris; video youtu.be/DbfWxmGsh2M). Solaris generates an interface frame by frame, reacting to clicks, drags and keystrokes, instead of rendering HTML and CSS. It keeps recent visual history, predicts pixels, and expects an LLM or agent behind it for tool calls and state. Runway names persistence and latency as the bottlenecks (half a second of delay breaks the feel). Action: watch the 3-minute demo (youtube.com/watch?v=c2yCePPnrSA) and note where an always-generated UI would fail your own app: saved state.
