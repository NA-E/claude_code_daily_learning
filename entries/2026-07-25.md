**CONCEPT — Most AI agent ROI estimates skip the one step that tells finance whether it worked: costing it before you build.** *Source: The Nuanced Perspective / Chai and AI (thenuancedperspective+the-chai-and-ai-community@substack.com), "How to Cost an AI Agent Before You Build It," July 25, 2:30 AM UTC.* McKinsey's 2025 State of AI survey: 62% of organizations are experimenting with agents, but only 39% report any EBIT impact.
