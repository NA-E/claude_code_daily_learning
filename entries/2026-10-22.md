**CONCEPT — Production knowledge graphs: lock the ontology and reject quotes that are not verbatim.** Source: The Neural Maze, "Knowledge Graphs for Production RAG" (Lesson 4 of The Substack Brain, theneuralmaze.com/p/knowledge-graphs-for-production-rag). A fixed 5-node, 5-edge ontology (Publication, Author, Article, Claim, Topic); topics canonicalized in Python (NFKC slug plus an alias dictionary) so "RAG" and "retrieval augmented generation" land on one node; and a zero-token guardrail that drops any extracted claim whose quote_span is not a verbatim substring of the article.
