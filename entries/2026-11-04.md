**CONCEPT — Pragmatic Engineer essay contest winner: Neil Kakkar went from 8 to 20 PRs a month to 200+, with an AI agent as the only reviewer.** Source: The Pragmatic Engineer (newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/how-software-engineering-is-changing; 125 submissions, $10,000 prize).
