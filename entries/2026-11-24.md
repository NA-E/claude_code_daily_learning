**NEWS — Lean and Mathlib maintainers certified Alpöge's Claude-assisted Riemann proof on Saturday with "full confidence," after ~6 weeks of work. One last statement-match check was still running.**
Source: The Rundown ("Claude helps solve math's biggest open problem"); The Neuron; The AI Break. The proof exists only as 17M lines of machine-checkable Lean. Alpöge wrote that Claude "flew, and danced, and sparkled near that finish line." The Clay Institute's $1M Millennium Prize has been attached to the problem since 2000. Why it matters: it's the first Millennium Problem to fall to a machine-checked, AI-assisted proof. Known gaps: the score doesn't compare across labs' datasets, and chain-of-thought adds capability without lowering perplexity. Separately, METR says the length of task models can finish is doubling about every 3.6 months. Why it matters: it's the first public capability scale from a frontier lab, tied to a proposed industry agreement. Who: anyone tracking release timing or AI policy. Action: none.
