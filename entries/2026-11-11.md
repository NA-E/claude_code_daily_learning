**TOOL — airgap-check finds hidden internet calls in "local" AI apps.** Source: The Neuron "AI Skill of the Day" (github.com/humanethq/airgap-check). On 10 unseen open-source AI repos, 9 had first-use downloads (tokenizers, embedding models, browser parts) and it flagged all 9, with 4 false alarms and 5 missed downloaders; numbers are self-reported. Commands: `airgap-check /path/to/repo` (static scan, reads .env key names not values); `airgap-check --run --image my-app:latest --run-cmd "python3 -m pytest tests/smoke_test.py" /path/to/repo` (Docker, network off); `airgap-check --fix /path/to/repo` (writes a Dockerfile block to prefetch assets). Why it matters: an app that looks offline can still leak or hang. Who: anyone deploying to locked-down machines. Action: run the static scan on any repo you call "local".
