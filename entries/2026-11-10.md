**CONCEPT — Co-Design Bench: the best of 10 models met 69.9% of hidden criteria but fully completed 26.4% of 273 app-editing tasks.** Source: The Neuron (arxiv.org/abs/2610.35138) and The AI Break (credits Alibaba and GPT-6 Astra as the best model). Experts wrote 2,331 hidden acceptance criteria; a task counts only if every criterion passes. A judge checked each criterion against evidence, then a separate verifier challenged every pass. Why it matters: "mostly done" is not done; it extends the grader problem from 11-07 and 11-09's Realtime-Bench (44% at real response time). Who: anyone shipping agent-written code. Action: define pass/fail criteria before the agent starts.

**WORKFLOW — Checklist-then-skeptic prompts.** Source: The Neuron "AI Skill of the Day" (the newsletter notes the paper did not test this; it is their adaptation). Step 1: paste "Before doing this task, turn my request into a numbered checklist of acceptance criteria. Each item must be something I could mark pass or fail... Show me the checklist and wait for my OK." Step 2: after the work, open a fresh chat and say "Your job is to prove the work wrong... Mark each item FAIL, PASS, or UNCERTAIN. Only mark PASS if you tried to break it and couldn't." Step 3: send only FAIL and UNCERTAIN items back. Why it matters: it copies Co-Design Bench's judge-plus-verifier grading by hand. Who: any Claude Code user. Action: run it on your next pull request.
